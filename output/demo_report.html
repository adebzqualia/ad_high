A filled cell contains a nonzero numeric value, including a rate. Blanks, zero, text, booleans and errors are invalid. A formula without a saved result is unknown. The overall rate is unknown whenever any expected cells are unknown; “known-cell rate” covers only the measurable cells. Coverage is the share we can measure. Totals use cell counts, not averages of percentages. Only matched, readable pairs enter the filling calculation.
